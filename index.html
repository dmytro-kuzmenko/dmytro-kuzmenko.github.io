CV Scholar GitHub LinkedIn X

My name is Dmytro and I am a machine learning researcher working on human-compatible embodied intelligence at the intersection of reinforcement learning, robotics, foundation models, and human-robot interaction. I am particularly interested in intelligent systems that can continuously learn, adapt, and make decisions in complex, real-world environments that people share.

I am an Assistant Professor at NaUKMA where I teach across machine learning, reinforcement learning, computer vision, and NLP. Alongside teaching, I am building and supervising student research projects. I am pursuing a PhD in Computer Science and am currently a visiting PhD researcher at AIRLab, University of Turin, working on assistive and socially intelligent human-robot interaction.

If you would like to discuss research, collaboration, or related opportunities, feel free to reach out by email.
